Shipment Pricing Formula

×

Our prices are calculated dynamically based on current system parameters:

Base Price: $5.00 (Fixed starting cost)
Weight Factor: $2.00 / kg (Multiplied by shipment weight)
Address Surcharge: $10.00 (Applied if delivery is to a home address)
The Formula:
Price = Base + (Weight × Factor) + [Surcharge]
